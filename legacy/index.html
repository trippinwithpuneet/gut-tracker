Gut Reset

Log what you eat and how the gas behaves. After a week of cutting the usual suspects, add them back one at a time — the tracker will point at what's actually driving the smell.

Days 1–7
Eliminate
Cut eggs, dairy, legumes, onion/garlic, cruciferous, outside chicken & oil. Eat plain.
Days 8–14
Reintroduce
Add back one group every 2 days. Watch which one spikes the smell.
